Screen share moves

Your screen recording floats on a wallpaper, zooms to what you talk about, and your webcam lives in a card bottom-left. Pick a move to jump to it, or play the whole demo. Written in projects/screen-share/share.js.


    

  


  

    
Camera moves

    
Talking head: the speaker is a window that morphs to a picture-in-picture or a side rail. The footage is only panned and scaled inside it, so it never re-crops. Written in components/camera.js.

Transitions

    
Cover a cut: the cut sits at the middle of the transition. Hover to play. Most cuts need none; use them for section changes.

    

  


  

    
Springs

    
Every card, zoom and camera move uses one of three springs (__hfGlass.ease.*). Press a curve to replay it.
