# Sample reel script (placeholder text). Lines starting with # are notes and are not timed.
Most reels lose people in the first three seconds.
Here is why: they open with "hi, I am John" and the scroll continues.
Open on the problem instead. Say the thing your viewer is already thinking.
Second, change something on screen every two or three seconds, a card, a number, a cut.
Third, keep every caption on screen long enough to read: half a second of calm after it lands.
One reel, one idea, one action.
Follow for the checklist.
